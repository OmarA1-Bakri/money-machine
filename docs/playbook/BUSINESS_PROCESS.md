The source sequence is preserved without reproducing its text. A concept is exactly one identity niche by one base category; qualification, dedupe, truth-only merchandising, maturity, repair, cull, and multiply rules are binding.

## Cross-cutting invariants

- Four qualification checks are each scored `/10`; below `30/40` blocks build.
- A matching identity and category, or roughly 70% title overlap, fails dedupe and requires reconcepting.
- Tests and simulation never publish, purchase, spend, or message.
- No demand verdict occurs before 30 live days; defects create repair immediately.
- Completion, domain events, and successors commit atomically; provider effects use separate idempotent/reconcilable ledgers.
